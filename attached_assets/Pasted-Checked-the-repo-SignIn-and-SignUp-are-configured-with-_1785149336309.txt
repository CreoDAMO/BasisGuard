**Likely cause, and why it's different from the earlier white-screen issue:** the error boundary I added earlier *is* live in the code now — I checked, `main.tsx` has it wired in. If that widget's async initialization fails — most likely the same Clerk domain/proxy validation issue as before, just surfacing on the interactive component instead of the initial page load — it fails silently, past the error boundary, leaving you looking at the app's own dark theme background with nothing rendered on top of it. That reads as "black screen," not "white screen," which fits.
